Sounds of Maple Line

Recorded ambience is mixed with procedural train, wind, door, brake, footstep and wiper sounds. Recordings are bundled with the game and load when sound is enabled.

  • Forest: “Park ambiences — park_ambience_birds.wav” by Thimras.
    Original recording · CC0 1.0.
    28-second excerpt, filtered, level-adjusted and encoded as MP3. This is general forest atmosphere, not a species-specific Japanese bird recording.
  • River: “Water flowing sound” by erxer1.
    Original recording · CC BY 3.0.
    Excerpt from 8–35 seconds, filtered, level-adjusted and encoded as MP3. Used with attribution; no endorsement implied.
  • Rain: “Rain (loopable)” — 1.mp3 by Ylmir.
    Original recording · CC0 1.0.
    27-second excerpt, filtered, level-adjusted and re-encoded as MP3.
  • People: “Busy Room Ambience / People talking in background #2” by Breviceps.
    Original recording · CC0 1.0.
    Public preview excerpt from 2–26 seconds, filtered for distant indistinct voices, level-adjusted and re-encoded as MP3.

Wind, roof rain and summer insects

  • Tree rustle: “Wind in the Trees” by Joseph SARDIN.
    Original recording · CC0 1.0.
    18-second excerpt from 20 seconds, filtered, mixed to mono, gain-adjusted and encoded as MP3. Recorded in Rambouillet, France; used as general foliage texture.
  • Roof rain: “Rain on car roof” by Joseph SARDIN.
    Original recording · CC0 1.0.
    14-second excerpt from 15 seconds, filtered, gain-adjusted and encoded as mono MP3. Recorded inside a road vehicle; adapted as a sheltered roof texture.
  • Summer cicadas: “Tokyo-cicadas.wav” by xserra.
    Original recording · CC BY 4.0.
    18-second excerpt from 12 seconds of the public MP3 preview, filtered, mixed to mono, gain-adjusted and re-encoded. Recorded in a Tokyo park; species unspecified. No endorsement implied.

All recording loops receive a short crossfade at their boundaries during playback.
