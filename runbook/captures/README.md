# Runbook game captures

Captured from the local Maple Line game on 22 September 2026. Each JPEG is 2560 × 1440 pixels, exported directly from the WebGL canvas at JPEG quality 95. The capture session temporarily disabled adaptive resolution and hid the HUD. Camera poses were changed only in the isolated browser session; game source was not changed for the captures. These images are still views, not evidence of animation or performance.

The captures added on 23 September 2026 for chapters 38–49 (`clips`, `pose-fix`, `cast-models`, `dialogue-lines`, `blocked-shot`, `before-after`, `episode-poster`) are contact sheets and paired captures that agents produced during that session's character, framing, video and visual passes, at their original sizes (1280–1920 pixels wide) and compressed below 300 KB. `concept-sheet` and `key-frame` are AI-generated concept art (openai/gpt-image-2.5-flare), not game captures; their `note` in `subjects.json` says so, and the runbook shows that note in place of the capture size.

The captures added on 25 September 2026 for chapters 50–57 are a 1600 × 1000 Character Studio capture (`studio`), side-by-side frames from the 9:16 renders of The 17:42 (`seated`, `talking`, `night-faces`, `dub`) and the trailer's shot contact sheet (`trailer-sheet`), each below 200 KB.

`subjects.json` assigns each inspected feature a capture, caption and highlight rectangle in image percentages. New framing requires checking those rectangles again. The build copies these files into the site's assets folder. The earlier embedded captures remain in the original teaching artifact, but the published runbook uses this set.
