The render loop limits reflection and shadow refresh to roughly 20 Hz while refraction follows capture calls. It also restricts spatial visibility and nearby shadow casters. Frame draw counters include the additional passes because `renderer.info.autoReset` is disabled and reset explicitly. Verify these details in the current loop before interpreting measurements.

## Invariants to preserve

- Terrain, track grade, train spacing, tunnel clearance, and stations belong to the existing route/simulation. Decorative generation must not silently change their geometry or physics.
- Riverbed, banks, surface, and vegetation exclusions share the river profile.
- Authored level edits are visual placements, with stable IDs and undo/redo; they are separate from Jev's world-transformation lifecycle. See [level building](../LEVEL-BUILDING.md) and [world builder](../WORLD-BUILDER.md).
- Dispose resources according to ownership. A retired chunk may release its own buffers; it must not dispose a shared material still used by neighboring chunks.
- Runtime control state stays in Zustand. Three.js objects and frame-by-frame transforms stay outside serialized state.
